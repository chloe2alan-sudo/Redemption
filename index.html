A careful approach

Every cryptocurrency case is different.

Recovery depends on factors such as the type of scam, the blockchain involved, how funds were transferred, whether assets reached a regulated exchange, and how quickly the incident is investigated.

✓We do not guarantee that cryptocurrency can be recovered.
✓Any success-rate figure should be evaluated against the specific cases and methodology used to calculate it.
✓Legitimate assistance should be transparent about limitations and lawful about its methods.
Protect your information

Never send your seed phrase, private key, password, or two-factor authentication codes to a recovery service or anyone else who asks for them.

Be cautious of anyone demanding additional money while promising guaranteed recovery. A legitimate service should clearly explain what it can and cannot do.
